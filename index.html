    Under the hood

    The call is a real Android incoming call: a ConnectionService registers it with addNewIncomingCall(), exact alarms get through Doze, a full-screen intent wakes a locked screen with the task name, priority and snooze, and a boot receiver reschedules every pending alarm after a reboot.

    Quick add examples: "Call dentist tomorrow at 4:30 pm", "Review PR in 45m", "#work call mum !!!". A bare "7" prompts you to choose AM or PM. Built with Compose, Room, Flow and Hilt, using a deterministic parser rather than AI.
