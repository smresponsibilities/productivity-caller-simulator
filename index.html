Productivity Caller vs Traditional Reminder Apps (TickTick, Todoist, Google Tasks)

Push notifications get swiped away alongside spam. Productivity Caller makes your phone ring with a real incoming call on the native Android call screen, using the OS Telecom framework. Everything runs locally on your device: no cloud, no account, no AI service. Try it next to TickTick, Todoist and Google Tasks.

The Core Problem: Notification Blindness

Task reminders compete with email, social updates and promotions. A push banner can disappear with a swipe even when the task still needs attention. Repeated alerts become background noise.

Productivity Caller uses the Android Telecom API, a ConnectionService and TelecomManager.addNewIncomingCall() to register a genuine incoming call event locally. The Android call experience asks you to answer or decline. It does not place a cellular call or require a remote caller.

Android behavior varies. Call presentation and lockscreen wake depend on Android version, device manufacturer, phone-account setup, permissions and notification settings. Telecom integration alone does not guarantee a full-screen wake or bypass Do Not Disturb.
